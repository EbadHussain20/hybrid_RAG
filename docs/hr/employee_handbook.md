# Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

**Department:** HR
**Version:** 5.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-01

## Vacation and Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year during their first two years, increasing to 20 days after two years of tenure. Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance.

### Vacation Approval

Vacation requests of 3 days or fewer require only direct manager approval. Requests of more than 3 consecutive days require both manager and department head approval, submitted at least 3 weeks in advance to allow for coverage planning.

Unused vacation days may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year; anything beyond that is forfeited unless local law requires payout.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, which do not require advance approval but must be logged in the HR portal within 48 hours of return.

## Remote Work

For remote and hybrid work arrangements, refer to the Remote Work Policy, which governs eligibility, approval, and equipment.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to act with integrity, avoid conflicts of interest, and report violations of this handbook to HR or through the anonymous Ethics Hotline.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave must be requested at least 30 days before the expected start date where possible.
