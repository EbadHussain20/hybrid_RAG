# Remote Work Policy

**Department:** HR
**Version:** 2.1
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-10

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to request a remote work arrangement. Roles requiring physical presence (lab technicians, on-site facilities staff) are not eligible.

## Approval Process

Remote work requires written approval from the employee's direct manager, submitted through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance. Fully remote arrangements (more than 3 days per week) additionally require sign-off from the department head.

Manager approval is not required for occasional remote work of one day or less per week; employees may self-schedule this with a calendar notification to their manager.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time $300 home office stipend for approved remote employees. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure home network per the Employee Security Policy.

## Core Hours

Remote employees must be reachable via Slack and email between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local timezone, regardless of their broader working hours.

## International Remote Work

Working remotely from outside the country of employment for more than 30 consecutive days requires approval from HR and Legal due to tax and labor law implications.
