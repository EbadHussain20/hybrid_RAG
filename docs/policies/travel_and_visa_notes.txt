INTERNAL NOTE: Travel & Visa Support (Policies)
Last Updated: 2025-05-30

NimbusForge provides visa application support (fee reimbursement and an invitation letter) for employees traveling internationally for business purposes, coordinated by the Finance team's travel desk in line with the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

Note: there is some inconsistency across older internal guidance about how far in advance visa support must be requested. The Global Mobility FAQ (deprecated, 2023) stated "at least 4 weeks," while the current Finance travel desk intake form states "at least 6 weeks." Employees should follow the 6-week guidance from the current intake form, since the 2023 FAQ has not been maintained.

Emergency travel with less notice may still be supported at the discretion of the Finance travel desk lead, but is not guaranteed.
