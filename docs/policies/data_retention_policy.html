Data Retention Policy

Department: Policies & Compliance
Version: 1.1
Last Updated: 2025-07-19

Retention Periods

  • Customer support tickets: retained for 3 years.
  • Financial records: retained for 7 years per regulatory requirement.
  • Employee records: retained for the duration of employment plus 5 years.
  • Security incident logs: retained for 2 years, per the Incident Response Plan.

Deletion Requests

Customers may request deletion of their personal data via the Privacy Portal. Requests are processed within 30 days, consistent with applicable data protection law. Backups containing deleted data are purged on the next backup rotation cycle, within 90 days.

Exceptions

Data subject to a legal hold is exempt from scheduled deletion until the hold is released by Legal.
