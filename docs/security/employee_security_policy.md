# Employee Security Policy

**Department:** Security
**Version:** 3.2
**Last Updated:** 2026-01-15

## Password Requirements

All employees must adhere to the following password rules for corporate systems:

- Minimum length of 12 characters, including at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one symbol.
- Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.
- Reuse of the last 5 passwords is prohibited.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all systems that store customer data.

## Account Lockout

Accounts are automatically locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. Employees must contact the IT Helpdesk to unlock their account.

## Security Incident Reporting Process

Any employee who suspects a security incident (phishing email, lost device, unauthorized access, malware) must:

1. Immediately disconnect the affected device from the network if malware is suspected.
2. Report the incident to security@nimbusforge.example within 1 hour of discovery via the Incident Reporting Portal.
3. Do not attempt to investigate or remediate the incident personally.
4. The Security Operations Center (SOC) will acknowledge the report within 30 minutes during business hours and assign a severity level (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
5. Critical incidents trigger the Incident Response Plan and require notification of the CISO within 15 minutes.

## Device Security

- All laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.
- Personal devices used for work (BYOD) must be enrolled in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) system before accessing company email.

## Data Classification

Data is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted. Restricted data (e.g., customer PII, financial records) may not be stored on local drives or personal cloud storage.
