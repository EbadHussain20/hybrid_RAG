# Customer Support SLA

**Department:** Product
**Version:** 1.5
**Last Updated:** 2025-10-22

## Response Times

- Critical (production down): 1 hour response, 24/7.
- High: 4 business hours.
- Medium: 1 business day.
- Low: 3 business days.

## Escalation

If an SLA is at risk of being breached, the support engineer must escalate to the on-call engineering lead defined in the Deployment Guide's on-call rotation.

## Beta Feature Exclusion

As noted in the Product Roadmap, features under the Beta Program are not covered by this SLA.
