# Annual Budget Planning Process

**Department:** Finance
**Version:** 1.2
**Last Updated:** 2025-08-14

## Timeline

Department budget proposals are due to Finance by October 15 for the following fiscal year. Finance consolidates proposals and presents a draft company budget to the executive team by November 15. Final budgets are approved by the Board in December.

## Variance Reviews

Each department head reviews actual-vs-budget variance monthly. Variances exceeding 10% must be explained in the monthly finance review meeting.

## Capital Expenditure

Capital expenditures over $50,000 require a business case and CFO approval regardless of which department's budget it falls under.
