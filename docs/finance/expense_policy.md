# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Department:** Finance
**Version:** 1.7
**Last Updated:** 2025-12-01

## Submission

Expense reports must be submitted through the Finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except with VP-level approval.

## Approval Thresholds

- Expenses under $100 require only manager approval.
- Expenses between $100 and $1,000 require manager and Finance approval.
- Expenses over $1,000 require Finance Director approval and must include a business justification.

## Travel

Economy class flights are standard for trips under 6 hours; business class may be booked for flights over 6 hours with manager pre-approval. Per diem for meals is $75/day domestically and $100/day internationally.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Alcohol at client dinners is reimbursable up to $50 per event; personal entertainment, traffic fines, and first-class upgrades are not reimbursable.
